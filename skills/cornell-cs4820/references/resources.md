# Resources for CS 4820

## Primary Textbook

- **Algorithm Design** by Jon Kleinberg and Éva Tardos (2005)
  - ISBN: 978-0321295354
  - Companion site: https://www.cs.cornell.edu/courses/cs4820/2026sp/
  - Covers all core CS 4820 topics with clear proofs and exercises

## Supplementary Textbooks

- **Introduction to Algorithms (CLRS)** by Cormen, Leiserson, Rivest, Stein (4th ed., 2022)
  - Comprehensive reference for data structures and algorithms
  - Better for lower-level details and pseudocode conventions

- **Algorithm Design: Foundations, Analysis, and Internet Examples** by Michael Goodrich and Roberto Tamassia
  - More examples, good for visual learners

- **Algorithms** by Sanjoy Dasgupta, Christos Papadimitriou, and Umesh Vazirani
  - Concise and elegant treatment, free online draft

- **The Design of Approximation Algorithms** by David Williamson and David Shmoys
  - Deep dive into approximation algorithms (covers Module 7+)

## Online Course Materials

- **MIT 6.046J/6.006** — MIT OpenCourseWare algorithms courses
  - Lecture videos, problem sets, and solutions
  - https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-046j-design-and-analysis-of-algorithms-spring-2015/

- **Stanford CS 161** — Tim Roughgarden's algorithms course
  - Excellent lecture notes on Coursera and Stanford Online
  - https://timroughgarden.org/

- **Princeton Algorithms I & II** — Robert Sedgewick (Coursera)
  - Strong on implementation and visualization

## Practice Platforms

- **LeetCode** — Algorithmic problems tagged by technique (greedy, DP, graph, etc.)
- **Codeforces** — Competitive programming with editorial solutions
- **Kattis / Open Kattis** — University-style algorithmic problems
- **VisuAlgo** — Animated visualizations of algorithms (https://visualgo.net/)
- **Algorithm Visualizer** — Interactive visualizations (https://algorithm-visualizer.org/)

## NP-Completeness References

- **Garey and Johnson: Computers and Intractability** — The classic NP-completeness reference
  - Catalog of NP-complete problems with reductions
- **Scott Aaronson's NP-Completeness Primer** — Accessible introduction
  - https://www.scottaaronson.com/democritus/

## Papers and Advanced Reading

- **Christofides (1976)** — 3/2-approximation for metric TSP
- **Karger (1993)** — Randomized min-cut algorithm
- **Vazirani (2001)** — Approximation Algorithms (textbook)
- **Arora and Barak (2009)** — Computational Complexity: A Modern Approach (for 5820 advanced topics)

## Tools

- **Python** with networkx (graph algorithms), scipy (optimization)
- **JFLAP** — Automata and formal languages tool (useful for reductions)
- **Graphviz** — Graph visualization
- **CS Academy / GeoGebra** — Interactive math visualization
