**Topics**: Computational thinking, binary, algorithms, Scratch
**Lecture**: [Watch](https://cs50.harvard.edu/x/2024/weeks/0/)

## Week 3 - Algorithms
**Topics**: Searching, sorting, recursion, Big O notation
**Lecture**: [Watch](https://cs50.harvard.edu/x/2024/weeks/3/)
**Notes**: [Read](https://cs50.harvard.edu/x/2024/notes/3/)
**Problem Set**: [Algorithms](https://cs50.harvard.edu/x/2024/psets/3/)
**Key Concepts**:

- Selection sort (O(n²))
