# MIT 6.034 - Artificial Intelligence

Patrick Henry Winston's foundational AI course from MIT OpenCourseWare (Fall 2010). Covers the core ideas and techniques of modern artificial intelligence.

## Course Structure

### Part 1: Search and Rule-Based Systems
- **Search**: BFS, DFS, hill climbing, simulated annealing, beam search, branch and bound, A*
- **Rule-based systems**: Forward/backward chaining, conflict resolution, expert systems

### Part 2: Constraint Satisfaction and Logic
- **Constraint satisfaction**: Arc consistency, backtracking, forward checking, min-conflicts
- **Logic and planning**: STRIPS, situation calculus, partial-order planning

### Part 3: Probabilistic Inference
- **Probability**: Bayes' rule, joint distributions, naive Bayes
- **Graphical models**: Bayesian networks, Markov models, Viterbi algorithm
- **Decision theory**: Utility functions, decision trees, information gathering

### Part 4: Machine Learning
- **k-Nearest neighbors**: Distance metrics, weighted voting
- **Decision trees**: ID3, information gain, pruning, overfitting
- **Neural networks**: Perceptrons, backpropagation, deep learning
- **Boosting**: AdaBoost, weak learners, ensemble methods
- **Kernel methods**: SVMs, feature spaces

### Part 5: Vision and Interpretation
- **Computer vision**: Object recognition, scene understanding
- **Semantic interpretation**: Parsing, generation, knowledge representation

## Textbook

Russell, S. & Norvig, P. *Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach* (3rd or 4th ed.)
