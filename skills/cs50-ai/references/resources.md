## Tips for Success

1. **Start with the official materials** - They're well-designed
2. **Watch lectures at 1.5x speed** - Save time, rewatch difficult parts
3. **Code along** - Don't just watch, implement
4. **Use office hours** - CS50 staff are incredibly helpful
5. **Join the community** - Discord, forums, study groups
6. **Build projects** - Apply what you learn to personal projects
7. **Read papers** - Start with survey papers in areas of interest
8. **Stay updated** - AI moves fast, follow key researchers
