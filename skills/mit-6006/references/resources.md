## Recommended Study Order

1. Watch lecture → take notes
2. Read corresponding CLRS sections
3. Implement the algorithm
4. Solve 2-3 related problems on LeetCode/HackerRank
5. Review and optimize your implementation
6. Move to next lecture

## Tips for Success

- **Active learning:** Don't just watch — implement every algorithm
- ** spaced repetition:** Review old topics weekly
- **Teach others:** Explaining concepts solidifies understanding
- **Build intuition:** Trace through algorithms by hand before coding
- **Time yourself:** Practice solving problems under time pressure
